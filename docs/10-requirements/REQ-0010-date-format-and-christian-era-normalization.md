# REQ-0010: Date format consistency and Christian Era (CE) normalization for transaction date pickers

## Problem

When creating or modifying transactions in the income-expense / general transaction section (e.g. via the monthly recurring checklist or manual editing):
1. **Inconsistent Date Format**: General transactions generated by the recurring checklist used the Buddhist Era (BE) year (e.g. `2569-09-15`) from `tax_years.year` rather than standard ISO Christian Era (CE / Gregorian) date format (`2026-09-15`).
2. **Double BE Year Conversion (Year 3112)**: Thai date formatters (`formatShortDate`, `formatThaiMonthYear`) assume stored dates are in Christian Era and add 543 to convert to Buddhist Era. When given `2569-09-15`, they computed $2569 + 543 = 3112$, displaying corrupted dates like `กันยายน 3112` and `15 ก.ย. 12`.
3. **Date Picker Misalignment**: When modifying such a transaction in `TransactionForm`, HTML5 `<input type="date">` expects ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` (CE). Receiving year 2569 caused Chromium date pickers in Thai locale to malfunction or display year 3112 BE.

## Scope

1. **Date Utility Normalization**:
   - Centralize date parsing, formatting, and BE/CE conversions in `src/renderer/lib/dateUtils.ts`.
   - `normalizeDateToCe`: Normalize any `YYYY-MM-DD` string with year $\ge 2400$ to CE year (`year - 543`).
   - `normalizeYearMonthToCe`: Normalize any `YYYY-MM` string with year $\ge 2400$ to CE year (`year - 543`).
   - Safe `formatThaiMonthYear` & `formatShortDate`: Guard against double BE addition.
2. **UI & Forms**:
   - Pass normalized CE `yearMonth` (`2026-MM`) to `RecurringChecklist` in `Entry.tsx`.
   - In `TransactionForm.tsx`, normalize edit dates and form submission dates to CE.
   - In `RecurringChecklist.tsx`, format the header title in Thai Buddhist Era (`รายการประจำเดือน (กันยายน 2569)`).
3. **Backend Defense-in-Depth**:
   - In `transactions.ts`, `recurring.ts`, and `csv.ts`, normalize all incoming transaction dates and `year_month` values $\ge 2400$ to CE format.
4. **Database Migration**:
   - Add migration `006-normalize-transaction-dates.ts` to normalize existing transaction records and recurring monthly logs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: All transactions (both Tax-relevant and General) store dates in standard ISO Christian Era `YYYY-MM-DD` format (e.g., `2026-09-15`).
- **AC-2**: Month ledgers and transaction lists correctly display Thai Buddhist Era dates (e.g., `15 ก.ย. 69` and `กันยายน 2569`) without displaying year 3112.
- **AC-3**: Editing a transaction in `TransactionForm` loads and persists the date in standard CE ISO format without date picker corruption.
- **AC-4**: Migration 006 automatically normalizes existing database records with Buddhist Era dates.
